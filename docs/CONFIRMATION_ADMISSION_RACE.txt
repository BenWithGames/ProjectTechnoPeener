Confirmation and admission race reduction

Fast-channel receive is repeated once after a successful fresh send batch,
before sleeping. Each receive pass remains capped at 32 packets. Sender remains
a worker; authentication, duplicate/conflict handling and owner-thread application
are unchanged. Counters distinguish post-send passes and accepted receipts.

At native admission, an eligible prediction-budget rejection gets one immediate
late drain and retry. Native service is not repeated. The reservation gate and
fight/prediction checks are reevaluated. No grace sleep, forced catch-up loop,
clock increment outside the native advance routine, or extra prediction horizon.
The unchanged-state check in NativeClockAccess::finish remains authoritative.
This reuses the existing audited service/wait/advance ordering (3F5D20 and
3E2000); no new native offset or instruction replacement is introduced.

This targets missed opportunities which can leave one peer behind, not a general
peer phase-lock controller. It cannot fix genuinely late packets or remove all
corrections. Adaptive delay and all restoration/round guards remain unchanged.
Counters admission_receipt_rechecks/recovered plus existing phase and correction
journals must be compared in paired live reports before claiming reduced jitter.

Tests cover receipts during sends, admission-time arrivals, absent receipts,
failed drain, lost gate/eligibility, disabled prediction, excluded rejection,
and actual session budget/reservation integration. One native service and at most
one advance are required. Large test dispatch fixtures are heap allocated.
