Repeated owned replay (suite profile 14)

Extends the successful same-session reference replay with three bounded cycles.
Each captures four consecutive snapshots, retains supported dependencies, restores
and replays three original-input frames, verifies results and retires the window.
The next window starts 120 simulation frames after the previous window ends.
No snapshots are captured during that spacing interval.

Round/session/owner changes cancel or reject under existing rules. No replay crosses
a round boundary. A repeated frame or backwards frame during the spacing interval
is rejected. Partial completion does not pass. Failed restoration/replay retains
the existing restart requirement. Timing of the startup hold remains unchanged.

Evidence is immutable and separate per cycle: four captured snapshots, replay
expected/actual evidence, animation trace, inline RNG trace, and diagnostic values.
First-window snapshot filenames remain compatible with the single-window runner.
Failure evidence is separate from successful replay evidence, so a later failure
cannot be masked by the earlier archive's published state. No published evidence
is reused or overwritten by the game thread.

Profiles 12 (removal/revival) and 13 (single owned replay) retain their test targets.
Profile 14 does not require object removal, a wall break, idle inputs, or deliberately
wrong predictions. It is not continuous rollback or a latency reduction test.

Procedure: both install with SC6 closed, play one private warmup match, return to
lobby, start Steam-TestSuite.cmd on both PCs, wait for connection, then enter the
next match and play normally. Leave both games open through the 30-second
observation/result exchange. Collect both reports. Restart before another suite.
