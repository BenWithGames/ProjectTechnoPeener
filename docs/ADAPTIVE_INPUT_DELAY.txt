Adaptive input delay (supersedes earlier fixed-delay and worst-of-three notes)

Each PC proposes 0, 1 or 2 frames at safe round preparation. Both authenticated
offers are immutable and acknowledged, with both native holds confirmed before
activation. Both PCs select max(local proposal, peer proposal). Inputs still send
immediately and are consumed at sample frame + agreed delay. Delay is immutable
until the next round; the prediction limit remains three frames.

The dedicated ping worker measures five raw RTTs every three seconds. Selection
uses the fourth-smallest sample (nearest-rank 80th percentile), so one isolated
outlier cannot keep delay high indefinitely. Two elevated samples still count.
The maximum selection value from the two latest complete bursts selects the band.
A decrease therefore needs two complete supporting bursts; increases apply on
the next proposal. This is a jitter/responsiveness tradeoff, not a guarantee that
an excluded isolated spike cannot cause a correction or prediction-limit stall.

Thresholds stay unchanged: below 25 ms = 0f; 25 through 70 ms inclusive = 1f;
above 70 ms = 2f. Both PCs must support a decrease. The displayed ping remains
the minimum adjusted RTT; it is not the selection statistic. Thus 65 ms shown
on the overlay can still correctly coexist with 2f if repeated samples or the
other PC's measurements exceed 70 ms.

Missing/incomplete bursts, invalid chronology and unavailable peer evidence use
2f. At observation, the supporting burst must be within six seconds. A proposal
expires when the latest burst is over six seconds old or its predecessor over
nine seconds old; this allows the next three-second burst to be in flight without
an expiry flicker. No added startup wait or mid-combat delay changes are made.

Reports retain worst raw RTT and add adaptive_delay_selection_us,
adaptive_delay_complete_recent and adaptive_delay_evidence_reason (0 valid,
1 insufficient history, 2 incomplete/invalid burst, 3 invalid/stale chronology).
The proposal also checks freshness at read time. Per-round reports retain local
proposal, peer proposal and agreed delay. Existing verification, ownership cleanup,
synchronized startup, intro preparation and all round safety checks are retained.
