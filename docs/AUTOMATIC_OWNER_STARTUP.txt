Automatic startup owner-thread fix

Side detection previously read fight readiness on the control-poll thread, then required the evidence to belong to the native clock thread. This prevented automatic side selection even when compatibility and resource preparation succeeded.

The clock hook now publishes fight readiness alongside its clock observation before live routes exist. The control poll consumes that bounded-fresh observation. Round/generation, playback/result, local mask and producer-thread checks remain enforced; native admission still revalidates before activation.

Reports include automatic side selection status, producer/control/fight thread identities, observed mask and round/frame metadata. Low latency, exact verification, resource ownership and round safety remain unchanged.

Regression coverage includes cross-thread P1 selection, P2 selection, stale observation, wrong round/generation, playback/results and mismatched producer evidence. Actual two-PC activation still requires a live match.

Startup hook ordering and target retry update

All five native startup hooks are installed before agreeing on a target frame. After hook preparation the poll returns, so the next proposal uses a fresh clock observation. Queueing performs no hook installation.

Continuous startup uses version-2 boundary proposals with monotonic target revisions. A refreshed target preserves the verified clock-to-battle offset, clears acknowledgements and requires bilateral agreement. Old acknowledgements cannot authorize the revised target. Both PCs need this build; transport signatures reject the older startup protocol.

At most 16 target revisions are processed per match. A queued request can be withdrawn only while waiting or after an exact missed-target rejection before preparation. Atomic state transitions prevent a concurrent native prepare from winning after withdrawal. Callback shells stay alive for in-flight hook readers. No retry is permitted after preparation begins or across round identity/generation/event changes. Existing native preparation, exact verification, low latency, ownership and round safety checks remain active.

Report fields: continuous_start_hooks_ready, continuous_start_target_revision, continuous_start_retries, continuous_start_rejection, continuous_start_observed_frame, continuous_start_last_rejection, continuous_start_last_observed_frame.
Rejection codes: 1 deadline expired; 2 wrong owner thread; 3 clock read failed; 4 clock identity changed; 5 target frame missed; 6 dependency source unavailable; 7 rearm wiring failed; 8 native preparation failed (see stage); 9 exception.

Validation covers newer proposals, crossed proposals, stale acknowledgements, invalid mappings, overflow, safe withdrawal with a previously loaded callback, missed-frame withdrawal and refusal to withdraw after resource preparation. Live two-PC activation remains to be verified.
